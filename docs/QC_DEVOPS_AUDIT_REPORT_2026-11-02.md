## Executive Summary

Following the 74-finding quality-plan sweep (`2a79e72` through `994d220`), all standard gates pass (`cargo test` 716/716, `vitest` 1323/1323, `tsc`/`eslint`/`cargo fmt` clean, Playwright E2E 73 passed / 1 skipped / 0 failed, TruffleHog 0 verified secrets).

A deeper adversarial QC and DevOps audit across four specialized tracks uncovered **38 actionable findings** (2 Critical, 12 High, 16 Medium, 8 Low) that static linters and existing happy-path tests did not catch:

1. **Rust Backend & Effects Engine (9 findings: 2 High, 5 Medium, 2 Low)** — Unvalidated `dock_window_appbar` size parameter wrapping `u32` to ~4.29 GiB and ignoring multi-monitor origin; SAM3 timeout channel desynchronization returning stale frames to subsequent callers; `sam3_video_predictor` bypassing `with_sam3` auto-restart; `usize_param` allowing `0` at `step_by`/`%` sites when called outside `clamp_params`; unvalidated `ExportOptions.fps`; missing frame-buffer length checks in `blend_mask`; temp-file leaks on panic/error paths.
2. **React/TypeScript Frontend & WebGL2 Engine (11 findings: 5 High, 6 Medium)** — Three effects marked "accurate" (`anaglyph`, `chromatic_aberration`, `glitch.slice_shift`) baking a hardcoded 1000 px width assumption into UV offsets; CPU exact-preview freezing `animTime` at `0` when paused (diverging from WebGL preview at playhead); `loopMode: "pingpong"` never reversing direction and jittering at `outPoint`; autosave persisting the *processed* `previewDataUrl` instead of `originalDataUrl` (double-applying effects on restore); passive `onWheel` listener ignoring `preventDefault()`; perpetual 60 fps RAF loop in `ManifestAudioEngine`; unsafe non-null assertions in `ExportPanel` and WebGL object creation; stale `savedPreviousTex` handle across resize in `EffectChain`.
3. **Python SAM3 Bridge, DatamoshLib & Sidecar Tooling (10 findings: 3 High, 7 Medium)** — `cmd_refine_mask` computing IoU between original-resolution input masks and downscaled SAM3 backbone masks (`ValueError` crash on images > `MAX_SAM3_DIM`); predictable temp filenames in `mosh_cli.py` colliding across concurrent runs; locale-dependent `subprocess.run(..., text=True)` decoding without `encoding="utf-8"`; `tomato.py` `bstream_until_marker` missing markers at chunk offset 0 (`> 0` instead of `>= 0`) and propagating `None` on malformed AVI; empty-frame `IndexError` and `chunkSize=0` `ValueError` in `tomato.py` and `basic_modes.py`; infinite `while` loop in `pymodes.py` `echo` when a stream has no P-frames; non-string `auth_token` crashing `sam3_bridge.py` outside its `try` guard; `DEVICE == "cuda"` checks missing `cuda:N`.
4. **DevOps, CI/CD, Release & QA Infrastructure (8 findings: 2 Critical, 3 High, 3 Medium)** — `.github/workflows/release.yml` guaranteed to fail across all 4 matrix legs (`externalBin` binaries gitignored, never fetched, and Windows-only); dead updater pipeline (`tauri_plugin_updater` unregistered in `lib.rs` with no `plugins.updater` config while `commands.rs` and `release.yml` invoke/build updater artifacts); `ci.yml` and `secret-scan.yml` ignoring the `dev` integration branch; CI clippy missing `--all-targets`; root cause of `[drag-drop] Failed to register listener {"err":{}}` in E2E runs (`__TAURI_INTERNALS__.metadata` missing in `tauri-mock.ts` + non-enumerable `Error` serialization in `logger.ts`); local TruffleHog scripts missing `--exclude-detectors=Lob`; stale security/audit docs in `docs/`.
